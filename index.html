Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about using Sparkle.

What can I pay for on Sparkle?

You can buy airtime and data, pay cable TV and electricity bills, purchase exam pins and use other supported services.

How do I fund my wallet?

Wallet funding will be connected to a verified payment provider such as Monnify.

Can I become a reseller?

Yes. Sparkle is designed to support reseller pricing, referrals and API access.

Are my transactions secure?

Sparkle is designed so wallet balances are controlled by the secure backend rather than by the browser.
