Type the sender name you are approved to use. Sender IDs must contain 3–11 letters only and are subject to network approval.
Supported format: 080…, 234… or +234…0 recipients
Maximum 905 characters0 / 905
Please review Sparkle Bulk SMS rules

Networks may block messages containing bank or payment-company names, international brands such as WhatsApp or Facebook, OTP or verification-code wording, bank credit/debit alert wording, or long digit sequences. A year such as 2026 is allowed, and amounts should use commas (for example, 5,000). Messages to DND numbers may not be delivered. These restrictions apply before delivery, and Sparkle cannot guarantee delivery to every number.

🛡️

Only message recipients who have agreed to receive communication from your business. Do not use Bulk SMS for unsolicited spam, impersonation, fraudulent alerts or verification-code delivery.
